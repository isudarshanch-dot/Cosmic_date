A Quiet Transmission

To the Star
I Couldn't Keep

The stars drift slowly.

Chapter I — Before You

There was a version of me
that didn't know your laugh.

He slept a little easier.
Dreamed a little smaller.
Loved a little less.
I don't think I'd ever want
to become him again.

Chapter II — Thank You

Thank you...

For the conversations that made
ordinary days feel extraordinary.
For every smile that reached me
before your words did.
For unknowingly becoming
the safest place my heart had ever found.

Chapter III — The Truth

Somewhere along the way...

I stopped hoping to impress you.
I just hoped you'd stay.
That's when I realized
this wasn't a crush anymore.
It had quietly become love.

Chapter IV — The Hardest Answer

When you told me...
"I could not reciprocate
your feelings."

You weren't cruel.
You were honest.
And honesty is kinder
than promises made out of pity.

Chapter V — What Broke

People think heartbreak
is hearing "no."
It isn't.

It's waking up the next morning...
...and realizing the future you've been visiting
in your imagination no longer exists.

Chapter VI — If Love Was Enough

I used to believe that
if someone loved deeply enough...
the universe would eventually
bend in their favor.

Maybe it doesn't.
Maybe love isn't measured
by whether it is returned.
Maybe it's measured by whether
it remained kind after it wasn't.

Chapter VII — My Favorite Universe

In another universe...

Maybe our timing was different.
Maybe your heart arrived
where mine already was.
Maybe we would have laughed
about how obvious it all seemed.
But this universe chose differently.

Final Chapter

You were never mine to lose.
But I will always be grateful that,
for a little while, I got to know you.

I hope someone loves you
as gently as I wanted to.
SCROLL
End of transmission.
Thank you for being part of my universe.
